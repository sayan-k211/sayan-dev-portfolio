About

I'm Sayan Khadka. I grew up in Nepal, in the shadow of the Himalayas, and I'm now based in Tauranga, New Zealand, where I graduated from Toi Ohomai in 2026 with a Bachelor of Applied Information Technology. I build web and mobile applications. My final year project was a voice activated Android app for two conservation researchers, taken from first requirements through to a signed release I installed on their own phone. The part I'm proudest of is rarely the feature. It's the diagnosis, working out why something actually broke instead of changing things until the symptom disappears. Alongside that I make travel and lifestyle videos for a YouTube channel. Editing turned out to be closer to writing software than I expected. Both come down to deciding what to leave out.
